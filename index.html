Deliver Like a Pro

Your complete step-by-step guide to mastering Amazon deliveries — whether you're just starting out or looking to level up your game.

Trusted by 1,000+ drivers

Onboarding

First Steps

  • Complete your background check & drug test.
  • Attend the orientation session (in-person or virtual).
  • Set up your Amazon Flex / DSP app credentials.
  • Watch safety & delivery training videos.
  • Get your uniform, badge, and device.
Pro tip: Take notes during orientation — small details save time later.

Must-Know Policies

  • Delivery time windows & priority packages.
  • Customer notes & access codes.
  • Returns & undeliverable procedures.
  • Safety protocols (seatbelt, handbrake, etc).
  • App navigation & scanning basics.
Remember: Always log out of the app when not in use.

Morning Routine

Pre-Departure

  • Arrive at the station 15–20 min early.
  • Hydrate & eat a light breakfast.
  • Check your schedule & route map.
  • Inspect your device & portable charger.

App Check

  • Sign in to the delivery app.
  • Verify your route & stop count.
  • Download offline maps (if available).
  • Check for any route updates.

Mindset & Safety

  • Stretch your neck, shoulders, and back.
  • Check the day's weather forecast.
  • Set a positive intention for the shift.
  • Ensure your phone is fully charged.
Pro: A 5-min stretch reduces fatigue by 30%.

Loading & Delivery

Smart Loading

  • Sort packages by stop order (use the driver aid number).
  • Place first stops near the side/back doors.
  • Heavy items on bottom, light on top.
  • Use shelves & totes to separate zones.
  • Keep a clear path to the cargo area.

Scan & Verify

  • Scan each package during loading.
  • Check for damaged or missing labels.
  • Note any oversized or special-handling boxes.
  • Double-check the total package count.
Warning: Unscanned packages cause delays — scan every single one.

Loading Efficiency Checklist

  • 1. Group by zone (A, B, C, D) or stop number.
  • 2. Place envelopes & small boxes in the front seat (safe zone).
  • 3. Use bungee cords or nets to secure tall stacks.
  • 4. Keep the handbrake & gear shift area clear.

Road & Vehicle Issues

Vehicle Problems

  • Flat tire? Call roadside assistance immediately.
  • Engine warning light — pull over safely and report.
  • Low fuel — plan your refueling stops.
  • Brake issues — do not drive; call for help.

Navigation & Traffic

  • Use the app's reroute feature for delays.
  • Check traffic updates before each leg.
  • If a road is closed, find an alternate route.
  • Stay calm in heavy traffic — safety first.
Emergency: Save dispatch & support numbers in your phone.

What to Do in an Accident

  • 1. Check for injuries — call 911 if needed.
  • 2. Move the van to a safe spot if possible.
  • 3. Report the incident to dispatch immediately.
  • 4. Take photos of the scene and damage.
  • 5. Complete the incident report in the app.

End of Route

Returns & Undelivered

  • Scan all undelivered packages as "return."
  • Sort returns by reason (access, no response, etc).
  • Place returns in designated totes/bins.
  • Log each return in the app.

Final App Steps

  • Complete all required app prompts.
  • Submit your delivery completion report.
  • Check for any pending customer feedback.
  • Sign out of the delivery app.

Wind-Down

  • Debrief with your dispatcher (if applicable).
  • Note any issues or route improvements.
  • Hydrate and have a snack.
  • Prepare for the next shift.
Reflect: What went well? What could be better?

End-of-Day Van Check

Inspection Checklist

  • Remove all personal items and trash.
  • Check for any packages left behind.
  • Inspect for damage (scuffs, dents, glass).
  • Test lights, horn, and indicators.
  • Check tire pressure and fluid levels.

Refuel & Clean

  • Refuel if below ¼ tank (DSP policy).
  • Wipe down high-touch surfaces.
  • Vacuum or sweep the cargo area.
  • Report any maintenance issues.
Final step: Take a photo of the van odometer and fuel level.

Daily Maintenance Log

  • 1. Record mileage & fuel used.
  • 2. Note any mechanical concerns.
  • 3. Log cleaning & sanitization.
  • 4. Submit the van check report.

Delivery Completion Rate (DCR)

DCR represents the percentage of packages successfully delivered out of the total assigned to you.

How is DCR Calculated?

If you take out 1000 packages in a week and successfully deliver 970, your DCR will be 97%.

Target DCR

To maintain strong performance, aim to deliver at least 99% of your assigned packages each week.

Balancing Performance Metrics

DCR and DNR DPMO together account for 50% of your overall performance score (25% each). To succeed in both areas:

  • Attempt direct contact with the customer when delivery isn't straightforward
  • Leave a “We missed you” card when no one is available
  • Always follow the customer's designated safe-place preferences

What Impacts DCR?

DCR is only impacted when a delivery cannot be completed and is marked under certain statuses, such as:

  • Customer not available (no safe place approved)
  • Business closed
  • Address inaccessible or incorrect

Reattempting Deliveries

When you are unable to complete a delivery earlier in your route, revisit the address at the end of your shift to check if the customer is now available. This reattempt often takes less time than returning to the depot and increases your DCR.

Did Not Receive (DNR DPMO)

DNR, also known as Concessions, is the metric representing shipments customers claim were never received, despite being marked as delivered.

Calculation

Concession Amount ÷ Number of Packages You Delivered × 1,000,000

Root Causes of Concessions

  • Doorstepping packages: Leaving packages on the doorstep or in an unsafe location without consent.
  • Delivering to neighbors without consent: This could result in theft or confusion.
  • Customer abuse: Some customers falsely claim non-receipt.
Most Common Cause: Doorstepping without permission. Always call or text the customer and agree on a safe delivery solution.

Best Practices

  • If you leave the package in a customer's selected safe location: leave a “We Missed You” card and contact the customer to confirm.
  • If you can't contact the customer and no safe place is provided: mark it as “Customer unavailable,” make a second contact attempt, and keep the parcel for reattempt or return.

Delivery Success Conditions (DSC)

DSC measures how accurately deliveries are completed based on Delivery Workflow Compliance and Preference Honour Rate.

DSC replaces DNR DPMO and ensures deliveries use the correct process, scan types, locations, and proof of delivery.

Key DSC Requirements

Attended Deliveries

If selecting Delivered to Household Member, the parcel must be physically handed to a person at the address.

  • Only select this option when a customer or household member accepts the parcel directly.
  • If left in a safe location (e.g., behind bin, porch), do not select Household Member.

Group Stops

  • Deliver group stops one by one.
  • Only link stops together if they are for the same exact location/address.

Swipe to Finish Location

  • Swipe to finish near the actual delivery location.
  • Any swipe completion over 25 metres from the geopin may be flagged.

Proof of Delivery (POD)

  • Photos must be well-lit, clear, and not blurry.
  • The parcel must be visible in the photo.

Customer Safe Place Preferences

  • Use the customer-selected safe place option (e.g., Back Door, Shed, Porch).
  • Never select a safe place option that displays a yellow warning triangle.
Key Reminders:
  • False scans are one of the biggest contributors to DSC failures.
  • Always ensure scan type, POD photo, and delivery location match the actual delivery action taken.

Contact Compliance (CC)

CC ensures delivery associates make appropriate attempts to contact customers when a package cannot be delivered.

Achieving Compliance

To maintain an excellent CC score, associates must attempt contact in at least 98% of undelivered scenarios.

  • Scan the package at the delivery location.
  • Select the question mark (?) icon in the top-right corner.
  • Choose “Unable to Deliver.”
  • Select the most appropriate reason code.
  • Initiate contact via text message or phone call.

Accepted Contact Methods

  • Text Message: Automatically fulfills the CC requirement upon sending.
  • Phone Call: Must be answered by the customer or proceed to voicemail.
Key Reminders:
  • Each customer must receive an individual contact attempt, even at shared addresses.
  • Skipping steps or using shortcuts will result in a failed CC record.
  • When making a call attempt, you MUST let the call ring through — if you hang up it will count as a fail.

Customer Escalations (CE)

CE represents instances where a customer contacts Amazon to report an issue related to a delivery experience.

Escalation Timing

Customer Escalation data is subject to a four-week delay. This provides DSPs the opportunity to investigate and, if appropriate, appeal the escalation.

Preventing Escalations

  • Follow all delivery procedures accurately and consistently.
  • Respect customer property and preferences.
  • Maintain a professional and calm demeanor at all times.
Important Safety Protocols
  • If a customer engages in aggressive or inappropriate behavior, report it to your DSP manager immediately.
  • In any situation involving physical threats or violence, contact emergency services first, then your DSP manager.

Protection Through Proper Procedure

As long as you are consistently following the correct delivery procedures, Amazon will not uphold a customer escalation by default. If a customer complains but your actions were compliant, the escalation will not impact your record.

Customer Delivery Feedback (CDF)

CDF is the percentage of customers who leave you a positive or negative review after a delivery is made.

Once a delivery is made, customers receive an email notification with options to give a thumbs up or thumbs down. Alternatively, customers can leave a review through the Amazon app.

Preventing a Poor CDF Score

  • Be kind and friendly
  • Contact the customer if they are not home
  • Adhere to their specified safe location
  • Leave a “We missed you” card to inform the customer about the delivery status
  • Avoid actions that may upset the customer, such as doorstepping or walking across their lawn

If you find yourself chatting with a customer, ask them to leave you positive feedback!

Notify of Arrival (NOA)

NOA is the best all-round process that can improve your scorecard by sending a simple text message before each delivery.

How to Send an NOA

  1. Press the '?' Icon in the top right corner of your device
  2. Press 'Text Customer'
  3. Press 'Notify of Arrival'

Benefits of NOA

  • NOA counts toward the CC metric — the more NOA you send, the more it covers any mistakes on CC.
  • If you send 100 NOA and fail 1 CC, your score will be 99% — putting you back in the Fantastic bracket!
  • NOA reduces DNR Concessions — proven data shows NOA helps achieve Fantastic in DNR DPMO.
  • Alerts customers you're on the way — sometimes they'll be waiting at the door, saving you time!

Lost on Road (LoR)

Lost on Road is the newest metric to come to the scorecard, designed to reduce the number of parcels lost whilst out in transit.

Best Practices to Prevent Lost Packages

  • When emptying bags and organizing them in your van, double-check that none have fallen out onto the ground or under the van.
  • ALL parcels that cannot be delivered must be returned to either the remote debrief driver or the depot on the same day.
  • Parcels delivered but not completed on your device due to signal/map problems must be reported to your Manager.
  • Any packages marked as 'missing' during your day will be chased by your manager — if you find them while still on route, deliver them before you finish (within reason).
Important

All high value packages are under CCTV within the warehouse. When a driver marks one of these shipments as missing, an investigation is carried out by the loss prevention team.

E-Mentor — Safe Driving

E-Mentor is a third-party app that records and measures your driving throughout your route.

How to Use E-Mentor

  • Log in every single time you have a route at Amazon — failing to do so will impact adoption rate.
  • For best results, use a second device to prevent deductions for 'phone distractions' (even answering on hands-free impacts your score).
  • Hotspot your spare device off your main phone, log into E-Mentor, begin trip, wait about a minute, then disconnect from the hotspot.
  • Wedge your phone somewhere secure where it won't bang around (e.g., in the slit in the sunvisor or between the seats).
  • Log in before you leave home or at your first stop. Do at least 3 hours driving time before logging out and uploading your trip.

Score targets: 810 or above for Fantastic. If you get a score below 780, tell your manager — they have the chance to reset your account so the poor score doesn't impact you.
